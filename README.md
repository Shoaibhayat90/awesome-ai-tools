## 🤖 AI Assistants

- [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) — The all-rounder: writing, research, planning, and customer-message drafts. The free tier covers a lot.
- [Claude](https://claude.ai) — Excellent for long documents, careful writing, and analysis.
- [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com) — Google's assistant; a natural fit if your team lives in Google Workspace.
- [Microsoft Copilot](https://copilot.microsoft.com) — AI built into Microsoft 365 and Windows workflows.
- [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai) — Research assistant that answers with cited sources, so you can verify everything.
- [DisconfirmAI](https://disconfirmai.com/en) — AI investment research platform that looks for disconfirming evidence before you form a conviction: daily reading, filings research, change radar, and personal frameworks.

## ✍️ Marketing Content & Copy

- [Jasper](https://www.jasper.ai) — Marketing copy trained on sales frameworks: product descriptions, site pages, email campaigns.
- [Copy.ai](https://www.copy.ai) — AI copywriting workflows for blogs, ads, and sales outreach.
- [Canva Magic Studio](https://www.canva.com) — Magic Write drafts captions; Magic Design turns one idea into social posts, flyers, and presentations.
- [Descript](https://www.descript.com) — Edit video by editing the text transcript. No video skills required.

## 📱 Social Media Management

- [Buffer](https://buffer.com) — Schedule posts everywhere; AI helps draft and repurpose content.
- [Hootsuite](https://www.hootsuite.com) — Social scheduling with OwlyWriter AI for captions and post ideas.
- [Predis.ai](https://predis.ai) — Turns a product or idea into branded social creatives and short videos.
- [ManyChat](https://manychat.com) — Automates Instagram/Facebook DMs and comment-to-DM flows for social-first brands.

## 💬 Customer Support Chatbots

- [Tidio](https://www.tidio.com) — Lyro AI learns from your website and FAQs, answering the majority of queries instantly and handing off the rest.
- [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com) — Fin AI agent resolves routine questions and escalates complex cases to your team.
- [Zendesk AI](https://www.zendesk.com) — Multichannel support with automated ticketing for support-heavy operations.
- [Freshdesk](https://www.freshworks.com) — AI-assisted ticket management tied to a self-service knowledge base.
- [Zoho SalesIQ](https://www.zoho.com/salesiq/) — Customizable live chat with AI, ideal if you already use Zoho.
- [Chatbase](https://www.chatbase.co) — Train a Q&A bot on your own documents for a single site — fast and affordable.

## 📞 AI Phone Answering

- [Goodcall](https://www.goodcall.com) — AI agents that answer, qualify, and book callers, with flat pricing for high call volumes.
- [Smith.ai](https://www.smith.ai) — Blended AI plus live human agents for businesses that want a human fallback.
- [CallSphere](https://callsphere.ai) — AI agent that answers calls, books appointments, takes payments, and syncs your CRM.
- [Slang.ai](https://www.slang.ai) — AI phone answering purpose-built for restaurants and hospitality.
- [Answrr](https://tryanswrr.com) — 24/7 AI receptionist with appointment booking for growing teams.

## 📅 Bookings & Scheduling

- [Calendly](https://calendly.com) — Customers book straight into your calendar; AI handles rescheduling, time zones, and reminders.
- [Motion](https://www.usemotion.com) — AI calendar that auto-plans your day around tasks, meetings, and priorities.

## 📚 Bookkeeping & Finance

- [QuickBooks](https://quickbooks.intuit.com) — AI-assisted bookkeeping, invoicing, and expense tracking.
- [Xero](https://www.xero.com) — Cloud accounting with smart bank reconciliation and clean invoicing.

## 📧 Email Marketing & CRM

- [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com) — AI-assisted campaigns, audience segmentation, and send-time optimization.
- [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com) — Affordable email and SMS marketing with AI writing assistance.
- [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com) — Free CRM with AI email writing, call logging, and deal summaries; scales into full marketing automation.
- [Salesforce Agentforce](https://www.salesforce.com) — AI agents for sales and service, for teams outgrowing starter CRMs.

## 🔍 SEO & Visibility

- [Surfer SEO](https://surferseo.com) — AI-guided on-page optimization so your content actually ranks on Google.
- [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com) — Keyword research, site audits, and AI-assisted content briefs.

## ⚙️ Automation & Workflows

- [Zapier](https://zapier.com) — Connects the apps you already use; AI builds multi-step workflows from plain-English descriptions.
- [Make](https://www.make.com) — Visual automation builder for more complex, branching workflows.

## 🗣️ Meetings & Productivity

- [Otter.ai](https://otter.ai) — Transcribes meetings into summaries with action items. Hours back every week.
- [Fireflies.ai](https://fireflies.ai) — AI notetaker that joins your calls and logs notes straight to your CRM.
- [Notion AI](https://www.notion.so) — Writing, summaries, and Q&A inside your Notion workspace.
- [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com) — AI writing polish for emails, proposals, and posts.

## 🎨 Design & Branding

- [Canva](https://www.canva.com) — Drag-and-drop design with a full AI studio. The non-designer's design team.
- [Midjourney](https://www.midjourney.com) — High-quality AI image generation for brand visuals and ads.
- [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io) — Lifelike AI voiceovers for videos, ads, and phone systems.

## 🏪 E-commerce & Websites

- [Wix Symphony](https://www.wix.com) — A coordinated team of AI agents that runs day-to-day site tasks, captures leads, and follows up.
- [Shopify Magic](https://www.shopify.com) — AI product descriptions, email copy, and store assistance built into Shopify.

## Contributing

Contributions are welcome! Please open an issue or pull request to suggest a tool. A good suggestion:

- Is genuinely useful for a real business (not enterprise-only, not vaporware).
- Has a real product a non-technical owner can sign up for today.
- Fits one of the categories above, with a one-line, plain-English description.
